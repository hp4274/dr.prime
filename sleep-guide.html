Unboxing your dream pillow

When your Dr. Prime pillow arrives, you will see that it is in a vacuum-packed bag. Dr. Prime team has chosen this approach for environmental reasons, to make the package smaller. After taking the pillow out of the packaging, leave it for 6-8 hours to allow it to return to its normal shape & size.

Dr. Prime Standard Size: 54*36*12 CM (21.2x14.1x4.7 in)
1

Open External Package

Take your Dr. Prime package and pull along the dotted line, or carefully cut it open with a pair of scissors. Be sure not to damage the internal package.

2

Take Out Inner Package

Your Dr. Prime Pillow is protected by an additional vacuum-sealed package, pull it out of the external package.

3

Open Inner Package

Similarly to step one, cut along the dotted line while being careful not to damage the pillow inside the package.

4

Remove The Pillow

Carefully take out your Dr. Prime from the inner package.

5

Let It "Inflate"

Once removed, let your Dr. Prime firm up over the next 6-8 hours. During this time it will naturally take on its final shape.

2. Sleep Position Guide

Side Sleeping

Side sleeping is the most common and generally the best position for spinal health. Use the higher lobe. Keep your arms relaxed — not tucked under the pillow and not stretched straight out.

A pillow between your knees reduces lateral rotation of the hips and takes pressure off the lower back.

Back Sleeping

Back sleeping allows the spine to decompress naturally. Use the lower lobe. Avoid placing extra pillows under your head — additional height pushes the cervical spine into flexion.

If you experience lower back discomfort while back sleeping, place a pillow under your knees.

Stomach Sleeping

Stomach sleeping forces the neck to rotate 90 degrees to the side for hours at a time. No pillow can fully offset the spinal strain this creates. If you are a habitual stomach sleeper, transition to side sleeping gradually.

Start by placing a body pillow alongside you to prevent rolling to your stomach.
